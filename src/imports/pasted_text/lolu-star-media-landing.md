Design a clean, premium ONE-PAGE LANDING WEBSITE for a photographer/creative studio called “LOLU STAR MEDIA”.

Use the provided LOLU STAR MEDIA logo as the main visual identity.

The website should NOT feel complicated or like a large corporate website.

It should be a simple, elegant, cinematic landing page focused on:
1. The logo and introduction
2. A little about the photographer
3. What he offers
4. His projects/work
5. Contact and social media
6. Designer credit at the very bottom

Keep everything on ONE continuous scrolling page.

STYLE

Luxury photography
Minimal
Cinematic
Modern
Editorial
Black and gold
Premium but simple

Use large photography placeholders and generous spacing.

Do not use excessive cards, complicated navigation, dashboards, pricing tables, testimonials, or unnecessary sections.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES
--------------------------------------------------

This is a SIMPLE ONE-PAGE LANDING PAGE.

Do NOT create:
- Separate project pages
- Complicated booking systems
- Pricing tables
- Testimonials
- Multiple navigation pages
- Large dashboards
- Excessive cards
- Complex animations
- Unnecessary sections

The goal is to create a beautiful website that immediately communicates:

WHO LOLU STAR MEDIA IS
WHAT HE DOES
WHAT HIS WORK LOOKS LIKE
HOW TO CONTACT HIM

Photography should be the star of the website.

Use large images, black space, elegant typography and small gold accents.

Make it feel like a premium photographer's portfolio — simple, cinematic and expensive.

Create the design for desktop first and make it responsive for mobile.
